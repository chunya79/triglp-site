Practical product guide

How to prepare IGNYT sachets.

The current pictured ORYGN label gives a simple water-and-sachet routine. Always let the instructions on the pack you receive take priority if they differ from this page.

Reviewed 3 August 2026 · Check the supplied label before use

Three simple steps

01

Check the pack

Read the ingredients, warnings, serving size and preparation directions.

02

Add water

The pictured ORYGN label says to mix one sachet into 8 oz—about 240 ml—of water.

03

Mix well

Shake or stir thoroughly, then follow any additional directions on your current pack.

The supplied label controls: ORYGN's online listing and product imagery currently show different pack counts. Confirm the quantity and directions on the product delivered to you.

Before your first serving

Check suitability, not just convenience.

  • IGNYT contains salmon-derived ingredients.
  • Do not use general website claims to decide whether it is safe for a fish allergy.
  • Ask a qualified healthcare professional before use if you take medication, manage a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Do not use a supplement to replace prescribed treatment.

What this guide does not assume

Taking more than the current label directs should not be assumed to improve results. This guide also does not promise a particular timetable, weight change or medical effect. Record any unwanted reaction and seek appropriate advice.
