Evidence context

Ingredient research is not a prescription medicine.

ORYGN links IGNYT to research concerning ProGo and other salmon-derived bioactive peptides. That evidence can explain why the ingredient is of interest, but it does not automatically prove a particular outcome from the finished sachet at its marketed dose.

Important distinction: ORYGN discusses GLP-1, GIP and GLP-2 signalling. Shared pathway language does not make IGNYT medically equivalent to semaglutide, tirzepatide or another prescription medicine.

Before using IGNYT

  • Read the supplied label for ingredients, preparation directions and warnings.
