What IGNYT is

ORYGN describes IGNYT as a powdered supplement made with patented Norwegian bioactive salmon peptides. It comes in individual packets designed to mix into a daily drink. It is a dietary supplement, not a prescription GLP-1 or GIP medicine.

Important distinction: ORYGN discusses GLP-1, GIP and GLP-2 signalling. Shared pathway language does not make IGNYT medically equivalent to semaglutide, tirzepatide or another prescription medicine.

Before using IGNYT

  • Read the current pouch and sachet label for ingredients, preparation directions and warnings.
  • Because the ingredient is salmon-derived, seek qualified advice before use if you have a fish allergy.
  • Speak with a healthcare professional if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, managing a health condition or taking medication.
  • Do not change or stop prescribed treatment because of a supplement.
